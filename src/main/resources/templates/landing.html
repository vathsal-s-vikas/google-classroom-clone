How It Works

Our platform streamlines the academic workflow for all participants

01

Course Creation & Enrollment

Professors create courses and generate unique invitation codes. Students join courses using these codes, establishing a secure, controlled academic environment. Teaching assistants can be designated to help manage larger classes.

02

Assignment & Content Distribution

Instructors create structured assignments with clear requirements, deadlines, and supporting materials. Course content is organized into modules that can be revealed progressively or all at once, depending on pedagogical needs.

03

Submission & Collaboration

Students submit work individually or in teams, with automatic tracking of submission times and late penalties. For group assignments, team leaders can manage membership and coordinate unified submissions.

04

Evaluation & Feedback

Instructors review submissions, provide detailed feedback, and assign grades according to rubrics. Students receive notifications when evaluations are complete and can review their performance and instructor comments.

About Our Platform

Designed with educational principles and modern technology

Our Mission

To create a comprehensive learning management system that facilitates meaningful educational experiences through intuitive design, flexible workflows, and powerful collaboration tools.

Technical Foundation

Built on Spring Boot with a secure authentication system, responsive front-end, and robust database architecture. Designed to scale from small classrooms to large institutions.

Development Context

This platform was developed as part of an Object-Oriented Analysis and Design course project, applying software engineering principles to real-world educational needs.

Educational Philosophy

Our design emphasizes pedagogical flexibility, clear communication, and meaningful assessment to support diverse teaching approaches and learning styles.
